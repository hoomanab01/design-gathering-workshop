Wiki Loves Monuments Join the contest

Photograph a monument for Wikipedia

Wiki Loves Monuments is the largest photography competition in the world. All you have to do is upload a freely licensed photo of a monument in a participating country to Wikimedia Commons.

Your photo helps Wikipedia document monuments and heritage buildings. More than 50 countries run their own contest, each with its own monuments, partners and awards.

How your photo reaches the finale

Every country runs the competition over a one-month period between 1 September and 31 October.

  1. Photograph a monument

    Take the picture yourself. It has to show an identified monument in one of the participating countries.

  2. Upload it to Wikimedia Commons

    Upload it yourself, under a free licence, while your country's contest is open.

  3. Your country nominates ten

    Each national contest sends its ten best photos to the international finale. Entering your national contest enters you in the international one automatically.

  4. The international jury decides

    The jury judges the nominated photos and awards extra prizes to the best images from all participating countries.

10 photos per country

Five rules for the finale

To reach the international finale, a photo must be:

  • Of an identified monument
  • Self taken and self uploaded
  • Freely licensed
  • Uploaded in September or October of the contest year
  • Nominated through a national contest

You also need an activated email address on Wikimedia Commons. Each national contest adds its own rules and awards, so check your country's page before you upload.

Own photos only

What the international jury looks for

Three things, in no particular order.

  • Technical qualitySharpness, use of light, perspective.
  • Originality
  • Usefulness for WikipediaHow useful the image is for Wikipedia.

First, each juror rates every image from 1 to 5 stars, and the scores make a first ranking. The jury then talks it through and can take images out. Finally, each juror submits an ordered top 25, and the total of points sets the final ranking.

It started with one country

Wiki Loves Monuments began as a 2010 pilot in the Netherlands, which produced 12,500 freely licensed images of monuments. In 2025, almost 228,000 images came in from about 4,000 photographers in 56 national contests.

Wikimedia volunteers organise it, every September and October.

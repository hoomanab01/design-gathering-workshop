# 3 · Put it live on Netlify

1. Sign in at netlify.com first, so the site stays in your account.
2. Open **app.netlify.com/drop**.
3. Drag the `site` folder from this kit onto the page. Drag the folder, not the file inside it.
4. Netlify gives you a link. Open it on your phone.
5. Post the link where the workshop gallery is collected.

## Change it later

Make the change in Claude Chat, run `2-test-in-code.md` again, then in Netlify open your site, go to **Deploys**, and drag the `site` folder in again. The link stays the same.

## Give it a nicer name

In Netlify, open your site's settings and change its name. The link becomes `your-name.netlify.app`.
